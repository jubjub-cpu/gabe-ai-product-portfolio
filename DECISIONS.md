# Decisions

| Date | Project | Decision | Alternatives Considered | Reason | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07-16 | Portfolio | Build a single static central repository for the first five demos. | Five separate repositories immediately; improve the private Sporeborne repo. | Static central suite is easiest to review, works without paid APIs or a local Node/Git toolchain, and keeps the first release coherent. | Mature products can be split into separate repos later. |
| 2026-07-16 | Portfolio | Use deterministic demo logic instead of live AI calls. | Require OpenAI API key; mock only in docs. | Recruiters can run the portfolio without paid keys, and privacy claims remain truthful. | AI behavior is clearly labeled as simulated. |
| 2026-07-16 | Portfolio | Exclude real local documents and personal files from demos. | Reuse local downloads or screenshots. | Privacy, licensing, and professional credibility matter more than volume. | All examples are fictional and synthetic. |
| 2026-07-16 | Sporeborne | Do not include Sporeborne in the first five business-AI products. | Feature it as a creative portfolio repo. | It is private, game-focused, and outside the required categories, though it shows useful AI-assisted iteration. | Mentioned only in inventory classification. |
| 2026-07-17 | SignalOps | Split SignalOps into its own public repository. | Keep all demos only in the central portfolio. | SignalOps is the clearest operations-intelligence proof point and benefits from a focused README, release, issues, screenshots, and live demo. | It should be pinned alongside the central portfolio. |
| 2026-07-17 | Foundation audit | Rebuild DocuTrace first as the next standalone project. | ReviewFlow first; extract all four in original order. | DocuTrace has the clearest distinct workflow after SignalOps and can add citations, comparison, structured extraction, and verification. | Create `jubjub-cpu/doctrace-desk` before the other remaining foundation projects. |
| 2026-07-17 | ReviewFlow | Rebuild around approval-sensitive vendor exceptions. | Copy the generic embedded demo; archive it. | The orchestration pattern is valuable, but a specific policy workflow is needed to distinguish it from SignalOps and generic chat products. | Standalone work will add staged rules, events, and two human gates. |
| 2026-07-17 | FrameForge | Rebuild substantially as FrameForge Inspect with actual browser image analysis. | Preserve the metadata-only QA app as standalone; replace it entirely. | The current demo claims multimodal value without inspecting pixels. Local Canvas analysis can provide honest technical proof without a paid API. | The embedded demo remains historical evidence until the stronger release replaces it in featured curation. |
| 2026-07-17 | PilotMap | Preserve the embedded demo as an archive and replace its standalone slot with QueueCast Planner. | Expand generic AI opportunity scoring; build IntakeGuard. | Weighted opportunity scoring is repetitive and provides less employer evidence than forecasting, uncertainty, and scenario simulation. | Phase One gains quantitative decision-support coverage while the original remains accessible. |
| 2026-07-17 | DocuTrace | Release the rebuilt evidence workspace as standalone v1.0.0 and make it a recommended pin. | Keep the embedded search-only demo as the featured version; wait for live-model integration. | The standalone product adds cited retrieval, structured extraction, clause comparison, failure states, human verification, local and deployed browser tests, screenshots, issues, and independent documentation without a paid API. | Phase One is 2 of 5 complete; ReviewFlow becomes the next foundation project. |
| 2026-07-17 | ReviewFlow | Release the rebuilt vendor-exception workspace as standalone v1.0.0 and recommend it for pinning. | Keep the generic embedded demo as the featured version; add live model calls before release. | The standalone product demonstrates visible staged orchestration, policy checks, blocked evidence, editable drafting, two distinct human gates, event history, export, and deployed browser validation without external actions. | Phase One is 3 of 5 complete; FrameForge Inspect becomes the next foundation project. |
| 2026-07-17 | FrameForge | Release FrameForge Inspect as standalone v1.0.0 and preserve the metadata-only embedded app as archive evidence. | Promote the old handoff generator; require a hosted vision model. | Actual Canvas pixel analysis, generated bitmap fixtures, regional overlays, baseline comparison, local file handling, human decisions, and repeatable deployed tests provide stronger and more truthful multimodal evidence without transmitting creative assets. | Phase One is 4 of 5 complete; QueueCast Planner becomes the final foundation replacement. |
| 2026-07-17 | QueueCast | Release QueueCast Planner as v1.0.0 and preserve PilotMap only as an embedded archive. | Expand generic opportunity scoring; add another recommendation workflow. | QueueCast adds transparent forecasting, uncertainty, capacity simulation, quantitative visualization, and staffing tradeoffs without worker data or employment actions. | Phase One is complete; Phase Two candidate scoring begins immediately. |
| 2026-07-18 | EvalDeck | Release EvalDeck Studio as the first Phase Two product. | Start with event tooling; use a hosted judge model. | A deterministic evaluator makes formal rubrics, regressions, slice failures, disagreement, overrides, and release policy observable without API cost or customer outputs. | Phase Two is 1 of 5 complete; FlowReplay Console becomes next. |
| 2026-07-17 | FlowReplay | Release FlowReplay Console as the second Phase Two product. | Build a generic API request tester; connect to live endpoints. | Versioned contracts, retry classes, backoff, failure injection, idempotent no-ops, and reasoned dead-letter recovery show event-system judgment without credentials or production traffic. | Phase Two is 2 of 5 complete; VoiceGauge Local becomes next. |
| 2026-07-17 | VoiceGauge | Release VoiceGauge Local as the third Phase Two product without transcription. | Use stock voice clips; call a hosted speech API. | Generated WAVs plus real browser decoding make audio processing testable while avoiding voice privacy, biometric, and model-cost claims. | Phase Two is 3 of 5 complete; ColdChain Sentinel becomes next. |
| 2026-07-17 | ColdChain | Release ColdChain Sentinel as the fourth Phase Two product with deterministic multivariate telemetry. | Connect a live sensor feed; build a generic metrics dashboard. | Persistence, clear hysteresis, visible suppression, cadence gaps, and sensor-health evidence demonstrate monitoring judgment without real logistics or location data. | Phase Two is 4 of 5 complete; CivicCase Redact becomes next. |
| 2026-07-17 | CivicCase | Release CivicCase Redact as the fifth Phase Two product with local deterministic text processing. | Call a hosted PII model; use real case-note examples. | Exact offsets, confidence provenance, reversible decisions, manual spans, and value-free exports demonstrate privacy review without transmitting or fabricating real records. | Phase Two is complete at 5 of 5; final curation becomes the active milestone. |
